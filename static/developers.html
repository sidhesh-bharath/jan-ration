INDIA STACK-ALIGNED BY INTENT

Trust is part of the contract.

These are design principles for a production implementation, not a claim of government certification or live integration.

◉Consent first

Every citizen data read has a clear purpose, scope, and expiry.

⌁Minimum data

Use tokenized references. Do not retain Aadhaar, biometrics, OTPs, or payments.

↺Safe retries

Idempotency keys and event references prevent double deductions.

⌁Auditable

Record who initiated a change, what changed, and which state adapter answered.

⚡Resilient

Use circuit breakers, queues, read models, and clearly labelled last-known data.

अHuman-readable

Every machine status becomes a plain-language message and next action.
